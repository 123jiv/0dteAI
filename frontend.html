⚠️ Backtest integrity notes
  • Look-ahead bias: Midori avoids using future data when computing stats ✅
  • Survivorship bias: Free historical data may exclude delisted symbols ⚠️

How Midori Thinks

Transparency and education first. This page explains how Project Midori works, what data it uses, and what its limitations are.

1. What Project Midori Is

Midori is an AI-powered trading intelligence and coaching tool. It helps you understand market conditions, risk, and trade scenarios.

Midori is not a broker, adviser, or signal service. All outputs are educational scenarios to help you make your own decisions.

2. How Signals & Analysis Are Generated

Midori combines delayed market data (prices, volatility, moving averages, VIX, macro events) with an LLM to build structured scenarios.

  • Data in: Alpaca/Polygon prices, Alternative.me Fear & Greed, Finnhub economic calendar & news, FRED macro, basic fundamentals.
  • Reasoning: the AI looks for patterns, regimes, risk factors, and common technical structures.
  • Output: scenarios with entry/exit ideas, risk levels, and a HOW MIDORI DECIDED THIS explainability section.

3. What Confidence Scores Mean

Confidence is a historical pattern match score, not a promise. When Midori shows a % confidence, it should be read as:

  • Very High (90–100%): rare setup with strong confluence of signals.
  • High (70–89%): multiple signals aligned in the same direction.
  • Moderate (55–69%): favorable but not ideal conditions.
  • Low (40–54%): mixed signals; trade with caution and small size.
  • Speculative (< 40%): high uncertainty; only appropriate for very small, educational sizing.

These ranges are based on how similar setups behaved historically in backtests and factor studies. They are not guarantees.

4. Data Sources (All Commercially Licensed)

  • Alpaca Markets — real-time delayed stock quotes. Free commercial use. alpaca.markets
  • Polygon.io — market data and VIX. Free tier, commercial use allowed. polygon.io
  • Finnhub.io — financial news and economic calendar. Free tier, commercial use allowed. finnhub.io
  • FRED (Federal Reserve) — macro economic indicators. US Government public domain. fred.stlouisfed.org
  • Alternative.me — Fear & Greed Index. Free, commercial use allowed. alternative.me/fng
  • STOCK Act (Congress.gov) — congressional trade disclosures. US Government public domain. disclosures-clerk.house.gov, senate.gov
  • SEC EDGAR — institutional 13F filings. US Government public domain.
  • TradingView Lightweight Charts — MIT licensed open source charting. tradingview.github.io/lightweight-charts
  • OpenAI GPT — AI analysis and coaching. Commercial use permitted.

All sources are free for commercial use. Some data is delayed; Midori reflects these limitations in its commentary.

5. Known Limitations

  • No real-time options tape or order book — Greeks and IV are approximate.
  • Congressional trading data can lag real trades by up to 45 days.
  • 13F filings are quarterly snapshots and may be stale.
  • The AI can be wrong, biased by past regimes, or overfit to noisy patterns.
  • Past patterns never guarantee future results, especially in regime shifts.

6. Educational Purpose Statement

Midori is a learning tool. It helps you understand how markets, options, and risk management work in different conditions.

Nothing in this app is financial advice. Midori is not a licensed adviser, broker, or money manager.

Trade only with money you can afford to lose, and always do your own research. When in doubt, size down or step aside.
