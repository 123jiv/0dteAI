# UNSETLD voice sample, for approval

**Status:** draft. Approve the tone before I write all 600 lines. Each line in the real library will exist in a **Clean** and an **Unfiltered** version, and the user picks one in onboarding. Widgets and reminders stay clean on the lock screen by default.

This sample went through a writer pass and then a harsh editor pass. The editor rewrote 31 of 60 lines, cut near-copies of famous quotes (it ran about 25 web searches), removed cringe slang, and capped "bitch" at 2 uses, both aimed at the snooze button or the couch.

## Lanes (category names)

| Category | Lane | Why |
|---|---|---|
| discipline | **Show Up** | Plain words young guys already use for keeping their word to themselves. It carries 'never stop working' as everyday consistency. It replaces 'No Days Off', a gym-bro hashtag whose literal meaning ('never rest') contradicted our own rest-day rule. |
| money | **Bag Talk** | Slang for talking about money, so it reads as money habits and skills. It doesn't promise anyone they'll get rich. |
| gym | **Gym Rat** | A label guys already give themselves half-jokingly. It's about being the one who shows up, not about how your body looks. I cut 'Era' because 'X era' reads as 2023 slang and would get screenshotted ironically by 2026. |
| focus | **Lock In** | The most common Gen Z word for focus. It's short enough for a widget label and works as a verb in the lines ('lock the fuck in'). |
| confidence | **Back Yourself** | A plain phrase that won't date: trust yourself enough to act, then back it up. It replaces 'Aura Up', since 'aura' slang is already mostly used ironically and won't last as a category name. |
| habits | **Cut It Off** | People already say they 'cut off' a toxic ex or friend. Using it for habits is instantly clear and punchy, and the lines echo it ('or the old shit moves back in'). |

## Style guide (for the full 600)

VOICE: A friend who doesn't let you slack. Funny on the surface, serious underneath. Write to one person ('you'), never to a crowd.

DO
- Keep every line under 15 words and every notification under 12. Aim for 12 or fewer so it fits a widget. One idea per line, readable in one glance. A word is anything between spaces, so a hyphenated word or '4:13pm' counts as one.
- Be specific: the snooze button, the 2am cart, 12 tabs, the group chat, the notes app, the email you rewrote nine times. Specific is funny. Vague hype is cringe.
- Aim every swear at the reader's excuses, phone, couch, feed, cravings or habits. Use one swear per line, and rotate them so no word dominates. The current 30 unfiltered lines use fuck 7, shit 7, damn 5, hell 5, ass 5 and bitch 1.
- Use 'bitch' no more than twice in the whole library, and only in the 'the snooze button's / your couch's bitch' pattern, meaning an object or excuse is running you. Never aim it at a person.
- Write each clean line as its own line, not a bleeped copy of the unfiltered one. Never write 'f*ck'.
- Be tough on effort and kind on slip-ups. A relapse line says 'You're human, not a fuckup. Reset.', never 'you failed'.
- Use at most one slang term per line (lock in, the bag, cringe, NPC, send it, with your chest). Skip slang that dates fast (aura, era, sigma, rizz): within a year it only gets used ironically.
- Carry 'never settle' through ideas (settling, half-assing, staying comfortable), not the brand name. 'UNSETLD' appears only in the top rank and one onboarding line, never in daily lines or notifications.

DON'T
- Swear at or about women, other people or any group. No slurs, no 'real men', no alpha/sigma talk, no 'females', and never hype the reader by putting someone else down.
- Promise results: no 'you'll be rich', no 'six figures by 25', no 'get shredded', no 'this will change your life'. Talk about the work, not the payoff.
- Glorify training through pain or injury, skipping sleep or skipping meals. 'Show Up' means do what you said you'd do. It doesn't mean never rest.
- Mock ADHD, depression, anxiety or therapy, or call someone's struggle laziness.
- Copy or lightly remix famous lines (Goggins, Jocko, Nike, Brené Brown, Atomic Habits rules, viral TikTok sounds, meme templates like 'X with extra steps'). Motivational copy is so saturated that if a line sounds familiar, it probably is. Search every quote-like line before it ships.
- Write fortune-cookie lines, hashtags, emojis or ALL CAPS shouting.
- Sell inside the content. Lines and notifications never say 'shop' or 'drop'.

PRODUCT NOTES
- Unfiltered is a choice in onboarding. Unfiltered notifications show on the lock screen, so add a 'keep lock screen clean' toggle.
- Frequent profanity raises the App Store age rating, so answer Apple's age-rating questionnaire to match.
- Paywall prices come from StoreKit/RevenueCat when the app runs. That's why the headlines use {annual_price} and {lifetime_price} instead of typed prices.
- XP copy rewards showing up. Never write 'buy to earn'. Store perks should feel like a bonus, not the point of the app. Keep them occasional so discounts stay rare.
