## Trademark: file now

The "SET / SETTLED" space is active in 2026, and nationwide priority only starts at the filing date:
- **UNSET:** women's apparel, class 25, filed 2026-02-03 (serial 99631669). That's before your domain was registered, so it's worth a lawyer's look for class 25.
- **SETLD:** a finance app, class 9, registered 2026-09-01.
- **SETTLD:** filed 2026-09-13.

Suggested filing: **UNSETLD as a standard-character word mark** (it covers any font or logo), owned by your business entity:
1. **Class 25** (clothing): use-based, since hoodies and tees are already selling. Specimens: a photo of the neck label or hang tag, or a product page with add-to-cart.
2. **Class 9** (downloadable app for motivational content, affirmations, reminders, habit tracking): intent-to-use.
3. **Class 41** (online motivational content and personal-development material): intent-to-use.
4. Optional **class 35** (online clothing store, plus administering a customer loyalty program): protects the rank and rewards program under your name.

Fees are about $350 per class at the USPTO (check uspto.gov). A trademark attorney is worth it for class 25 because of UNSET.

Use the phrase as a tagline next to UNSETLD, but don't make it the app's name or try to register it.
