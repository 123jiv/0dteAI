> **Verified:** Seneca, *Letters to Lucilius* 13.4, translated by Richard M. Gummere (Loeb, 1917). The full sentence reads: "There are more things, Lucilius, likely to frighten us than there are to crush us; we suffer more often in imagination than in reality." The letter dates to about 63–65 CE, roughly 1,960 years ago, so "nearly 2,000 years old" holds. Optional small credit on the slide: *Seneca, Letters 13.4 (trans. Gummere)*.
